Plane scanner

Makes a 3D aircraft model from pictures or video frames: a side view (needed), a top or bottom view and a front view. Three-view drawings work best, photos taken square on from the side work too. Saves a .glb like the ones in models/.

1 · Pictures

0.0 s

Drop or paste (Ctrl+V) a picture onto the view. From a video, pick the frame with the slider.

2 · Cut out and line up

Brush size24
Colour tolerance (cut-out and wand)32

3 · Model

The orange outline in the picture is the model: it should cover the aircraft.
